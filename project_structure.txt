3. DATABASE STRUCTURE (MySQL)
------------------------------------------------------
database/
├── schema.sql                        (Creates Tables)
└── seed.sql                          (Inserts dummy data e.g. admin user)

TABLES REQUIRED (Based on schema.sql):
1. users (id, email, password_hash, role)
2. patients (id, first_name, last_name, dob, gender, phone, created_at)
3. appointments (id, patient_id, doctor_id, date, time, status)
4. diet_exercise_plans (id, patient_id, diet_plan, exercise_plan)
5. health_records (id, patient_id, diagnosis, treatment, record_date)
